## 1. Committee Leadership Mission

As a Team Lead at GDG on Campus NCTU, your core focus is on mentorship, clear sprint planning, and fair recognition.

### Key Responsibilities:
1. **Task Planning (`/tasks`):**
   * Define measurable deliverables with clear acceptance criteria.
   * Communicate expectations to members **before** work starts.
   * Manage the Kanban board (`Assigned` ➔ `In Progress` ➔ `Submitted` ➔ `Completed`).
2. **Recognizing Contributions (`/points`):**
   * Submit positive points (+5 Core Task, +3 Initiative, +2 Peer Support, +2 Docs, +1-3 Additional Contribution) with evidence links.
3. **Disciplinary Deductions:**
   * Communicate privately with members before submitting deductions.
   * Disciplinary recommendations enter `pending_review` and require independent HR approval.
4. **Monthly Evaluations (`/evaluations`):**
   * Grade assigned committee members across the 6 official criteria (30/25/15/10/10/10).
   * Toggle **N/A** for inapplicable categories to trigger automatic proportional weight renormalization.
5. **1-on-1 Feedback Sessions (`/feedback`):**
   * Conduct 15-minute coaching sessions at the start of each month to discuss growth goals.
