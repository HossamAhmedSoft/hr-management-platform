## 1. Welcome to Your Member Portal

Your personal dashboard is designed to support your development and track your achievements at GDG on Campus NCTU.

### Privacy Guarantee:
* Your evaluations, personal point records, and disciplinary notes are strictly private to you and authorized leadership. Peers cannot see your evaluations or points ledger.

---

## 2. Core Member Features

1. **Personal Dashboard (`/`):**
   * View your active tasks, personal points balance, and latest performance evaluation score and band.
2. **Tasks & Submissions (`/tasks`):**
   * View tasks assigned to you.
   * Update status to `In Progress` when starting.
   * Attach your deliverable link (GitHub PR, Figma file, Google Drive) and change status to `Submitted`.
   * Request deadline extensions or report blockers early without fear of penalty.
3. **Points Ledger (`/points`):**
   * Track your positive recognitions and cap usage across the 7 monthly categories.
   * Net balance tiers: Green (+10 or higher), Normal (0 to +9), Warning (-1 to -5), Red (-6 or lower).
4. **Appeals & Due Process (`/appeals`):**
   * If you receive a deduction you believe is inaccurate, you have **7 Egyptian working days** to submit an appeal with counter-evidence.
   * Appeals are reviewed by an independent HR Reviewer with a full point refund upon approval.
