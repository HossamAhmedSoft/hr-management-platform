## 3. NON-NEGOTIABLE IMPLEMENTATION RULES

1. Read and understand the entire available source policy before implementing its business rules.
2. Treat the official policy as the source of truth for all documented rules.
3. Never silently invent, change, or reinterpret policy thresholds, point values, deadlines, evaluation weights, or disciplinary procedures.
4. When the policy is ambiguous, preserve the documented information and expose the ambiguity as an administrator-configurable or explicitly unresolved policy item. Do not silently choose a consequential interpretation.
5. Keep positive and negative points separate from the monthly performance score.
6. A negative points balance must never automatically suspend, remove, or expel a member.
7. Never automatically finalize a disciplinary penalty based only on a numeric score.
8. Require evidence, appropriate review, and member notification wherever the policy requires them.
9. Record who performed each consequential action, when it occurred, what changed, and why.
10. Never expose one member's confidential records to another ordinary member.
11. Do not store credentials, API secrets, or service-role keys in frontend code.
12. Do not claim that a feature is implemented unless it actually works.
13. Do not overwrite an existing project blindly. Inspect the repository first and preserve valid existing work.
14. Do not use mock data as the permanent production data source.
15. Do not block the entire implementation because an optional integration is unavailable.

## 10. OFFICIAL POINTS SYSTEM

Implement a dedicated points ledger based on the source policy.

Positive and negative points are independent from the monthly performance score.

### A. Positive points

| Category | Points | Documented condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Completed Core Task | +5 | A core task is delivered on time and meets previously agreed requirements and quality standards. |
| Initiative & Innovation | +3 | A documented idea or implementation improves team workflows or saves time and effort. |
| Peer Support & Knowledge Sharing | +2 | Meaningful help or knowledge sharing improves team output. |
| Documentation & Archiving | +2 | Work is documented and archived professionally for future reference and reuse. |
| Additional Contribution | +1 to +3 | An additional contribution agreed upon with the Team Lead, valued according to its actual impact. |

### B. Negative points

| Category | Points | Documented condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Failure to Update Task Status | -1 | Status is not updated after a prior reminder, without notification. |
| Unjustified Delay | -2 | A deadline is missed without early notification or an agreed alternative. |
| Unexcused Essential Meeting Absence | -2 | Absence is reviewed after considering personal circumstances. |
| Disruption of Collaborative Work | -3 | Repeated failure to respond to important work messages causes actual disruption. |
| Incomplete or Deficient Delivery | -3 | Delivery is materially incomplete relative to documented requirements, without an adequate explanation. |
| Failure to Attend an Assigned Event or Responsibility | -5 | Failure to fulfill direct responsibility causes disruption to the event. |
| Inappropriate Conduct | -5 to -10 | Documented misconduct requiring neutral review and protection of both parties' rights. |
| Serious Violations | Investigation referral | Data leakage, taking credit for others' work, or damaging community digital systems. |

The source policy defines investigation referral rather than a fixed numeric penalty for serious violations. Do not invent one.

### D. Points ledger safeguards

## 12. MONTHLY PERFORMANCE EVALUATION — 100 POINTS

This evaluation must remain separate from the points ledger.

Implement the exact source-policy weights:

| Evaluation category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Task Completion | 30 |
| Quality & Standards | 25 |
| Reliability | 15 |
| Knowledge Sharing | 10 |
| Documentation | 10 |
| Growth & Initiative | 10 |
| Total | 100 |

Each category is rated from 1 to 5.

### Calculation

For every applicable category:

Category Score = (Rating / 5) × Category Weight.

Total Score = Sum of all applicable category scores.

When all six categories are applicable, the maximum total is 100.

Use decimal-safe calculations and a documented rounding rule. Keep unrounded values internally and round the displayed total consistently.

### N/A fairness rule

If a category cannot be assessed because the member had no actual opportunity to demonstrate it, mark it N/A rather than zero.

Recalculate the score proportionally using the remaining applicable weights:

Adjusted Score = Sum of Applicable Category Scores / Sum of Applicable Category Weights × 100.

If no categories are applicable, mark the evaluation as Not Evaluated rather than producing a misleading zero.

Record the reason for every N/A.

### Performance bands

Use the policy's thresholds:

- 90–100: Outstanding Performance.
- 75–89: Strong Performance.
- 60–74: Needs Improvement.
- Below 60: Coaching / Guidance Session.

Preserve the exact underlying numeric score. Display the band separately.

The score alone must never automatically trigger disciplinary action.

### Evaluation workflow

1. Evaluation period is opened.
2. The member completes a self-assessment where enabled.
3. The Team Lead rates the applicable categories and provides evidence.
4. The system validates ratings, weights, and N/A explanations.
5. HR reviews the evaluation.
6. The evaluation is finalized by an authorized person.
7. The member is notified.
8. A feedback session is scheduled and documented.
9. Development actions and follow-up dates are tracked.

Support draft, submitted, under review, finalized, and reopened states with explicit permissions.

Finalized evaluations must not be silently overwritten. Corrections require an authorized amendment with a recorded reason.
