# GDG NCTU HR PLATFORM — MEMBER USER GUIDE

**Target Audience:** Active Chapter Members, Contributors, and Workshop Attendees  
**Platform URL:** `https://hr-platform-cyan.vercel.app`  
**System Role:** `member`  

---

## 1. Welcome to GDG NCTU!

Welcome to Google Developer Groups on Campus at New Cairo Technological University!

The GDG NCTU HR Platform is your personal hub for tracking your chapter journey, discovering your tasks, seeing your points and recognitions, reviewing your monthly evaluations, and exercising your rights.

---

## 2. Signing In & Account Setup

1. **Accessing the Portal:**
   * Go to `https://hr-platform-cyan.vercel.app`.
   * Switch languages anytime using the **Language Button** (English or العربية) in the top navigation bar.
   * Switch between **Dark Mode** and **Light Mode** using the theme toggle icon.
2. **Signing In:**
   * Enter your registered chapter email address and your password.
   * If signing in for the first time, use the initial credentials provided by your HR onboarding officer.

---

## 3. Your Personal Dashboard & Privacy

* **Strict Privacy Isolation:**
  * Your dashboard is private to you.
  * Other general members **cannot** view your points ledger, your personal task scores, or your performance reviews.
* **Dashboard KPI Cards:**
  * **My Active Tasks:** Total tasks currently assigned to you.
  * **My Points Balance:** Net points earned this month and throughout the season.
  * **Evaluation Score:** Your most recent monthly performance rating and band.
  * **Appeals & In-App Alerts:** Live alerts about task reviews or point updates.

---

## 4. Managing Tasks & Submitting Deliverables (`/tasks`)

1. **Viewing Your Tasks:**
   * Navigate to **Tasks**. You will see only the tasks assigned to you or open to your committee.
2. **Starting Work:**
   * Drag your task or update the status dropdown from `Assigned` to `In Progress`.
3. **Requesting Help or Deadline Extension:**
   * If you face an academic conflict (exams, family event) or technical roadblock, reach out to your Team Lead **before** the deadline passes.
   * Asking for help early is valued and will never be penalized!
4. **Submitting Deliverables:**
   * Once your work is ready, paste your deliverable link (GitHub Pull Request URL, Figma design link, Google Drive folder) in the task submission field.
   * Change status to **Submitted**. Your Team Lead will review the deliverable.

---

## 5. Tracking Your Points & Monthly Caps (`/points`)

1. **Understanding Points:**
   * Points recognize proactive contributions:
     * Completing a core task: **+5.0 points**
     * Creative initiative or innovation: **+3.0 points**
     * Helping peers or sharing knowledge: **+2.0 points**
     * Writing guides or documentation: **+2.0 points**
2. **Monthly Category Caps:**
   * To keep competition healthy and prevent burnout, categories have monthly caps (e.g. Task Execution is capped at 9 points/month).
   * Check your **Points Ledger** page to view your current cap usage.
3. **Net Point Classifications:**
   * **Green Zone (+10 to +15 pts):** Outstanding contributor eligible for special chapter honors.
   * **Normal Zone (0 to +9 pts):** Healthy, consistent participation.
   * **Warning Zone (-1 to -5 pts):** Needs support; eligible for friendly check-in.
   * **Red Zone (-6 to -10 pts):** Disciplinary review required.

---

## 6. Monthly Performance Reviews & Feedback (`/evaluations`)

* On the 1st of every month, view your finalized evaluation score from the previous month.
* Your score is graded across 6 criteria (Task Delivery, Quality, Reliability, Teamwork, Documentation, Growth).
* **Performance Bands:**
  * **90–100%:** Exemplary Performance
  * **80–89%:** Strong Performance
  * **65–79%:** Needs Improvement
  * **<65%:** Critical
* Participate in your monthly 15-minute 1-on-1 feedback session with your Team Lead to discuss your development and goals.

---

## 7. How to File an Appeal (`/appeals`)

If you receive a point deduction or an evaluation score that you believe is inaccurate or unfair, you have the protected right to lodge an appeal:

1. **The 7 Working Days Window:**
   * You have **7 Egyptian working days** (Sundays through Thursdays; Fridays and Saturdays do not count) from the notification date to submit an appeal.
2. **Submitting the Appeal:**
   * Navigate to **Appeals** and click **"Submit Appeal"**.
   * Select the disputed point deduction or evaluation.
   * Explain your perspective clearly and attach any supporting evidence (e.g., chat screenshots, commit logs, medical notes).
3. **Independent Neutral Review:**
   * Your appeal is reviewed by an independent HR Reviewer who was not involved in the original decision.
   * If upheld, the points are completely refunded to your balance.
   * *Protection Guarantee:* Filing an appeal in good faith will **never** negatively affect your standing or result in retaliation.
